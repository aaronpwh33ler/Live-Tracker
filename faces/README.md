# Source faces

`source.jpg` is an AI-generated (StyleGAN, "this person does not exist") face of a **fictional person**. It comes from the MIT-licensed Hugging Face dataset [`javi22/this-person-does-not-exist-10k`](https://huggingface.co/datasets/javi22/this-person-does-not-exist-10k) (file `face (1).jpg`), resized to 512×512.

Put your own source faces here. Only use:

- AI-generated faces of fictional people, or
- photos of real people who have **given consent** to have their face used this way.

A clear, front-facing, evenly lit photo works best. It should show one face, with a little margin around the head.
